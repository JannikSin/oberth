# 0006. Keep the Career tab in Oberth, against the council's 4-1 vote

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-24
- **Deciders:** David, overruling the council
- **Tenet invoked:** none. A judgement about where a risk fails loudly.

## Context

The 2026-08-24 council voted **4-1 against** putting Career in an academic app.
Their argument: the highest-stakes lane in his life, the September 2026
Roundtable and the internship search, would be buried inside a homework tool and
would quietly stop being looked at.

That is a real risk and the council was right to name it.

## Options considered

- **Career stays in the Crystal app**, where it is already a tab, and Oberth
  links to it. Two places to look on any given day.
- **Career in Oberth with no special treatment.** Exactly the burial the council
  described.
- **Career in Oberth, with the nearest external deadline permanently in the
  masthead.**

## Decision

Career lives here, **and the masthead always shows the nearest external
deadline**, on every tab.

## Consequences

The council's risk now **fails loudly rather than quietly**: if the lane is being
neglected, the countdown is on screen saying so, which a buried tab would not do.

**What gets worse:** the same fact now appears in two apps and nothing keeps them
in sync. The council may still turn out to be right, and the honest test is
whether he opens the Career tab in a week containing no coursework deadline. If
the answer is no, this record gets superseded rather than defended.
